Privacy note

This note explains, in plain language, what happens to the details you give us on this site's forms: the workshop sign-up, the mentor interest form and the partner interest form. Last updated: 26 November 2026. Version: privacy-draft-0.2.

The forms are not connected yet, so right now nothing you type into them is sent or stored. This note is pending legal review and will be finalised before any form opens.

Who we are

Ketuna runs this site, the workshop and the programs. Privacy questions: sapreswapnil@yahoo.co.in. The legal entity responsible for your data, and its address, will be named here before any form opens. Until then, there is no data to be responsible for.

What we collect

  • All forms: your name and email address, that you ticked the consent box, which version of this note you agreed to, and when you sent the form.
  • Workshop sign-up: your phone number if you choose to give it, your current status, your college or organisation if you give it, which area interests you, which laptop you will bring, and how you heard about the workshop.
  • Mentor interest: your main area, years of experience, how and how much you would like to help, and anything you choose to write in the message box.
  • Partner interest: your organisation, its type, your role if you give it, your main interest and your message.

The site has no tracking, no advertising cookies and no analytics scripts. If that ever changes, this note will say so first.

Why we collect it

  • To reply to you, and for the workshop, to confirm your place and send setup instructions, venue details and day-of updates.
  • To plan: seats, setup help for each operating system, mentor availability.
  • To understand, in total and not person by person, which training areas people want.

We will not sell your details or share them for anyone else's marketing. We will only email you about other programs if you ask us to. The legal basis and consent wording will be confirmed in the legal review.

Where it is stored

When the forms open, your details will be sent over an encrypted connection to a form service or spreadsheet provider that stores them on our behalf. This note will name that provider, say where it stores data, and say who can see your details (only the people who run Ketuna's programs) before any form opens.

How long we keep it

Only as long as we need it for the purpose you gave it to us for, after which we delete it. The exact period will be stated here before any form opens. If you ask to hear about future programs, we keep your name and email until you ask us to stop.

Your choices

  • Ask to see, correct or delete your details at any time.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time. We then delete your details; for the workshop, this means you lose your place.
  • For these requests, for complaints and for any privacy question, write to sapreswapnil@yahoo.co.in.

Photos at the workshop

We ask before taking or sharing any photo of you.

Changes to this note

If we change this note, we update the date and version above. Every form records which version you agreed to.

This note is written for clarity, not as legal advice. It will be reviewed by a lawyer before any form goes live.
