Practice

Quiz bank

400 auto-graded questions, four per guide. Weekly quizzes in the terminal or on paper; every wrong answer comes back with a one-line reason.

Slide decks

One deck per teaching day, kept as text next to its lab and guides. The 12 common-core days are ready; track decks follow.

Admission test

60 minutes, 100 marks: digital logic, C and Python, a small Verilog module and a Python function. No AI tools. Format

Materials are not published for download. Colleges and companies can ask about access through the partner form.
