Projects

India-relevant product projects

Work in progress

Ten build-it-for-real projects that Ketuna is developing as the backbone of its capstones, mentored student work and partner collaborations. Each one starts as a board built from parts you can buy, and climbs towards a dedicated chip.

Status, honestly: these are concepts and plans, being shaped into project briefs. None is a finished product, and we claim no customers, partners or funding for any of them.

See the ten projects Work on a project with us

One ladder for every project

Every project climbs the same three steps, the way many real products grow: from available parts, to programmable logic, to silicon made for the job.

The ten projects

Each card says why the problem matters in India, where it starts, where it goes next, what you learn, and which Ketuna tracks it draws on: A design and verification, B physical design, C analog and mixed-signal (coming later), D embedded and edge AI, E systems and product.

Project 01

Low-cost, low-power drone controller chip

Work in progressAgricultureMobility
Why it matters in India
India is pushing hard for locally built drones, for agricultural spraying, survey, defence and delivery.
Start: board
A flight-controller board: IMU sensor fusion, motor PWM and ESC control, GPS and NavIC.
Then: FPGA / chip
A dedicated low-power control SoC.
What you learn
Real-time control, sensor fusion and CPU design.

ABDE Tracks: Design & verification, Physical design, Embedded & edge AI, Systems & product

Project 02

Simple, low-cost vision camera sensor

Work in progressAgricultureMobilityIndustry
Why it matters in India
Useful in agriculture, traffic, retail, security and factory inspection.
Start: board
A camera module and a small processor doing basic detection: motion, counting, defects.
Then: FPGA / chip
An image-processing pipeline in an FPGA, then an ASIC.
What you learn
Image pipelines, low-power AI and interfaces.

ABD Tracks: Design & verification, Physical design, Embedded & edge AI

Project 03

ADC array for a sensor-fusion controller

Work in progressIndustryHealth
Why it matters in India
Many Indian industrial and medical products need multi-channel, precise, low-cost sensing.
Start: board
A board with an external ADC array and a microcontroller doing the fusion.
Then: FPGA / chip
A mixed-signal chip: an ADC array plus a digital fusion engine.
What you learn
Analog and mixed-signal design, a skill that is rare and valuable in India.

ACD Tracks: Design & verification, Analog / mixed-signal (coming later), Embedded & edge AI

Project 04

NavIC receiver chip

Work in progressNavigationMobility
Why it matters in India
NavIC is India's own navigation system, and the government wants it in phones, vehicles and devices. Very few low-cost NavIC chips exist.
Start: board
A software-defined-radio (SDR) NavIC receiver on an FPGA.
Then: FPGA / chip
A baseband chip.
What you learn
Signal processing and receiver design, from FPGA to silicon, on a strategic and visible problem.

ABD Tracks: Design & verification, Physical design, Embedded & edge AI

Project 05

Smart energy meter SoC

Work in progressEnergy
Why it matters in India
Crores of smart meters are being deployed across India under the national rollout, and today they mostly use imported chips. Volumes are large and the users are clear: meter makers and electricity distribution companies (DISCOMs).
Start: board
A board with a metering front-end (ADC), a secure microcontroller and a communication module.
Then: FPGA / chip
One SoC with the metering front-end, a secure MCU and communication.
What you learn
Precision measurement, embedded security and communication.

ACDE Tracks: Design & verification, Analog / mixed-signal (coming later), Embedded & edge AI, Systems & product

Project 06

Battery management (BMS) IC for EVs and e-rickshaws

Work in progressEnergyMobility
Why it matters in India
Electric two- and three-wheelers and e-rickshaws are growing very fast, and battery safety problems are common.
Start: board
A BMS board: cell monitoring, balancing and protection.
Then: FPGA / chip
A cell-monitor ASIC.
What you learn
